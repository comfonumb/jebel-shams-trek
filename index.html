Trek plan – March 26–27, 2026

Mixed fitness team

10-14 hikers

Preparation Plan (2 Weeks Before Trek)

Week 1 – Conditioning

  • 10–12 km brisk walk or hike
  • Hill or stair climbing session
  • Strength training: squats, lunges, step‑ups
  • Core training (plank / balance)
  • Practice carrying a 5–6 kg backpack
  • Focus on hydration and sleep

Week 2 – Test Hike

  • 10–14 km test hike
  • 500–700 m elevation gain
  • Carry full backpack (6–7 kg)
  • Start before sunrise
  • Evaluate pace and endurance
  • Identify fast / moderate / slow hikers

Nutrition Preparation

  • Protein intake: ~1.2–1.6 g per kg body weight daily (eggs,yogurt,chicken,lentils,nuts)
  • Increase carbohydrates during final week (rice, bread, oats,bananas,potatoes)
  • Hydrate well (2–3 L daily), day Before-Drink 3–4 liters water through the day.
  • Avoid alcohol and sleep deprivation before trek

Trek Overview

The Jebel Shams W4 summit route is not a normal hike. It is long, exposed, poorly marked near the summit, and temperatures in early summer can exceed 30 °C at lower altitudes.The main risks are: Heat exhaustion / summit,Slow hikers causing late descent,Navigation difficulty near summit,Fatigue leading to falls on rocky terrain. Not everyone might summit, hence turn-around discipline is critical..

Turn‑Around Rules

  • Absolute turnaround time: 12:30 PM
  • If summit is more than 1 hour away at 11:00 AM→ descend
  • If water remaining is less than 50% → descend
  • No one hikes alone
  • Group leader decision is final

Strategy for Returners

  • Designate a safe checkpoint roughly 60–70% of the route
  • At that point leave the water cache (at least 10 L)

    • Water Management

    • Pre-Hydration-->Night before:2–3 L water,include salt/electrolytes. Morning before hike:500–700 ml
    • Do not wait until thirsty. Drink:150–250 ml every 15–20 minutes
    • 5L/person (Plain Water-3.5 L, Electrolyte Drink- 1L,Emergency Reserve- 0.5 L)
    • If drinking only water you risk Hyponatremia or muscle cramps. 1 packet of electrolytes every 1.5-2 hrs
    • Group Water Reserve-Carry additional shared water. (Recommended:10–12 L group reserve) Leader 1 carry extra 2 L, Leader 2 extra 2 L, 3 Strong hikers to carry 1 L extra water

Critical Waypoints to mark

  • Waypoint 1 – Trailhead / Start Point
    • Regroup before starting, check water, equipment and GPS tracking
  • **Waypoint 2 – Canyon Rim Section**
    • Distance from start: ~2 km, trail runs along the edge of canyon Purpose: • first pace check • ensure group spacing controlled
  • **Waypoint 3 – Midpoint Plateau**
    • Distance from start: ~5–6 km Elevation: ~2400 m This is an ideal long rest point (15–20 minutes). Also good location to: • assess slower hikers • decide if anyone should stop here.
  • **Waypoint 4 – Water Cache Point**
    • Distance: ~7 km Elevation: ~2600 m This is a strategic location because: • it lies on the return route • shaded rock pockets exist • summit push begins after this Recommended cache:10–15 L water
  • **Waypoint 5 – Navigation Confusion Zone**
    • Distance: ~8–9 km Terrain: • rocky plateau • faint trail • cairns scattered This is where hikers often lose the route. Strategy: • leaders regroup team • follow GPX track strictly • maintain visual contact
  • **Waypoint 6 – Summit Plateau**
    • Distance: ~10–11 km Elevation: ~3000 m True summit markers are sometimes unclear. Do not wander too far; visibility drops quickly in afternoon haze.

Emergency Warning Signs

  • Dizziness
  • Headache
  • Nausea
  • Muscle cramps
  • Confusion
  • Extreme fatigue

Expedition Details
